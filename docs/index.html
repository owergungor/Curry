ENGINEERED FOR FOCUS

Crafted for Windows Power Users

Experience notifications without disruptive popups pulling you out of your workflow. Every feature is designed with system performance, OLED preservation, and personal aesthetics in mind.

Ambient Screen-Edge Glow

Choose from 5 hardware-accelerated animation modes: Pulse, Sweep, Ambient, Comet, and Ripple. Fully tune border thickness, glow spread, duration, and opacity.

Native WinRT Notification Capture

Direct integration with Windows 10 & 11 native UserNotificationListener APIs. Captures alerts instantly with 0ms lag, zero polling, and near-zero background CPU consumption.

Auto App Scan & Custom Profiles

Auto-discovers installed desktop applications from Start Menu and Registry. Assign distinct glow colors, animations, duration overrides, and monitor targets per application.

Gaming & Fullscreen Suppression

Intelligently detects when you are immersed in full-screen games, video players, or presentations, automatically suppressing glows so your screen remains undisturbed.

OLED Panel Burn-In Protection

Built-in OLED Mode limits peak luminance, narrows edge spread, and modulates glow duration to protect high-end OLED and QD-OLED monitors from static pixel fatigue.

Multi-Monitor Coordination

Whether you use an ultrawide setup or multiple displays, route ambient glows to your primary display, the monitor housing your active window, or synchronize across all monitors.
